Confusable Pairs from Error Log & Glossary

Red-border white circle vs. Blue circle
White + red border = Interdiction (prohibition). Solid blue circle = Obligation (mandatory action).
Front vs. Rear fog lights (Entry #001)
Heavy rain = front fog lights only. Rear fog light is strictly for dense fog or heavy snow (blinds drivers behind in rain).
Carrefour à sens giratoire vs. Rond-point
Never assume uniform rules: read entry yield signs (Cédez le passage) and interior markings rather than road width.
Arrêt vs. Stationnement (Art. R110-2)
Arrêt = brief stop for boarding/loading with driver at controls. Stationnement = leaving vehicle parked.
Aire de repos vs. Aire de service
Aire de repos = parking, picnic, WC (no fuel guaranteed). Aire de service = fuel, food, and maintenance.
BAU vs. Voie de détresse
BAU = motorway shoulder for emergencies only. Voie de détresse = gravel escape lane strictly for brake failure.
