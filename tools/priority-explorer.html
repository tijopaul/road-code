ETG Scoring & Timer

40 questions, 20 seconds each, minimum 35 / 40 to pass. Each question has 1 to 4 correct answers—missing one or checking one wrong option gives 0 points.

Visibility Speed Cap

Even where 50, 80, 110, or 130 km/h normally applies, speed is capped at 50 km/h on all roads when visibility drops under 50 meters (Art. R413-4).

Absolute-Word Traps

Watch for toujours (always), jamais (never), seulement, and uniquement—they often signal an exception you must verify first.
