📜 What happened

    📈 Starting capital (capital initial)

    A new licence starts with 6 points. Without any offence it grows automatically each year until 12.

    ⚠️ An offence during probation stops the yearly increase. The maximum still grows, but your balance stays where it is. You get back to 12 only after 3 years without a new offence.
    🎓 Post-licence training (formation post-permis): 7 hours, taken 6 to 12 months after the licence. It shortens probation to 2 years (classic/supervised) or 1.5 years (AAC).

    ♻️ Getting points back

    • ⏳ Lost only 1 point? It comes back after 6 months without an offence.
    • 📅 Lost several points? Back to 12 after 2 years without an offence.
    • ⚖️ 3 years instead if the offence was a crime (délit) or a 4th/5th class offence (contravention de 4e/5e classe), and always 3 years during probation.
    • 🎓 Awareness course (stage de sensibilisation): up to +4 points, once a year. Not possible once the licence is invalid.
    📩 Below 6 points you get a registered letter inviting you to a course. In probation, losing 3 points or more makes the course compulsory.

    🚫 Zero points = invalid licence

    • Invalid for all categories; return the licence to the prefecture within 10 days.
    • Wait 6 months (1 year if it already happened in the last 5 years).
    • Licence held < 3 years: retake theory + practical tests. Held > 3 years: theory test.
    • Medical check and psychotechnical test needed.
    🚨 Driving with an invalid licence is a crime: up to 2 years in prison and a €4,500 fine.

    Note: some minor offences only bring a fixed fine (amende forfaitaire), with no points lost. A contravention costs up to 6 points; a délit costs 6 points straight away.
