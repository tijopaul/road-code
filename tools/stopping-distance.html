🧠 Exam rules of thumb

Reaction distance (1 s): take the tens digit of your speed × 3.
90 km/h → 9 × 3 = 27 m

Braking distance, dry: tens digit squared.
90 km/h → 9 × 9 = 81 m

Braking distance, wet: dry × 1.5.
90 km/h → 81 × 1.5 ≈ 122 m

⚠️ Double your speed → braking distance × 4. Speed has a huge effect!

✌️ The 2-second rule (distance de sécurité)

Keep at least 2 seconds between you and the vehicle in front. That covers your reaction time if it brakes suddenly.

🛣️ On the motorway: leave at least 2 white dashes of the edge line between you and the car ahead.

How to count it: when the car in front passes a fixed point (a tree, a sign), say "one thousand and one, one thousand and two". If you reach the point before you finish, you're too close!
